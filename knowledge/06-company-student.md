# Company and Student features

## My Work and Team Deadlines
In a Company workspace, My Work shows the tasks assigned to you, and Team Deadlines shows the whole team's deadlines.

## Assigning work to someone
Managers can choose a person in the For me menu next to the Add box, type @name, or write "assign the slides to Rahul". The person must be in the team list in Settings, Team.

## Workload
The Workload page shows each person's hours of work this week against their capacity, so a manager can spot overload and move work.

## Needs attention
Managers see Needs attention: work that is overdue, at risk, blocked, or waiting for approval, in one list.

## Approvals
When someone submits work for review, the manager can approve it or press Request changes.

## Roles
Owner and Admin can do everything. Managers can edit any task, approve work, reassign and see workload. Employees edit their own tasks. Guests only see shared work. Roles are set in Settings, Team.

## Exams
In a Student workspace, the Exams page lists upcoming and past exams with preparation progress.

## Study Planner
The Study Planner spreads study sessions for your deadlines and exams over the coming days using your study hours per day.

## Reports
Reports show your completion rate, on-time rate, overdue work, completed per week and estimate accuracy.
