# Deadlines, the radar and planning

## The Deadlines page
The Deadlines page lists every deadline with its status, priority and progress, and shows the Deadline Radar. Overdue deadlines appear first.

## Deadline Radar and risk levels
Each deadline gets a risk level: On track, Needs attention, At risk, Critical or Overdue. The level compares the work left (your estimate minus your progress, including other deadlines due earlier) with the hours you have before the due date. Every level shows its reason, such as "due in 2 days, 40% done, about 6 hours left". You can change the rules in Settings, Availability, Advanced: risk rules.

## Progress check-ins
On the Today page the app sometimes asks "How far along is ...?" with 0%, 25%, 50%, 75% and Done buttons. Your answer updates the progress so the risk level is based on real progress. Press Not now to skip.

## Estimates
Add an estimate (for example ~3h) so the radar and the planner know how much work is left. Deadlines without an estimate are listed under Needs an estimate.

## Backward planning and milestones
Open a deadline to see a plan that splits the work into daily pieces before the due date, so you start early instead of the night before. You can add milestones (smaller steps) to a deadline.

## Study hours and work days
Settings, Availability sets your study or focus hours per day and the days you work. The planner and radar use these to decide whether work fits before each deadline.

## Do deadlines ever move by themselves
No. The app and the assistant never move a hard deadline on their own. Moving a task plans the work for another day; the deadline stays. Changing a deadline itself always needs you to confirm it.

## Calendar
The Calendar page shows tasks and deadlines by day. Drag a task to another day to plan it for that day.

## Exporting to another calendar
Settings, Data, Export to calendar (.ics) downloads your deadlines for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

## Backup
Settings, Data, Copy backup copies all your data as text so you can keep it safe or import it later.
