# Deadline AI answers

## Asking Deadline AI any question
The Help tab of Deadline AI answers almost any question: general knowledge, science, maths, programming and debugging, AI and technology, English and writing, careers, business, study and planning. You don't need perfect spelling or grammar. Ask follow-up questions like "give me an example" and it remembers what you were talking about. Questions about how Deadline Tracker itself works are answered only from this help guide.

## Answers appear as they are written
With Google Gemini on, the answer shows word by word while it is being written, so you can start reading at once. If the AI is slow, the window says "Still working…" and tries again by itself a couple of times.

## Stopping an answer
While an answer is being written, the Send button turns into a Stop button (a square). Press Stop to end the answer. The part already written stays, marked "Stopped".

## Copying an answer
Press Copy under an answer to copy the whole answer. Code blocks also have their own Copy button for just the code.

## Let Deadline AI see my tasks
In the Help tab there is a switch called "Let Deadline AI see my tasks". It is off at first, and your tasks are never sent to Google Gemini while it is off. Turn it on (you are asked to confirm) and questions about your own work, like "what should I do tomorrow?" or "help me plan my week", are answered from your real open tasks. Only task titles, dates, priority and status are sent, never notes. Turn it off again any time with the same switch. General questions are answered the same way whether it is on or off.

## Can Deadline AI add or change my tasks
No. Deadline AI in the Help tab can read your task list (only if you allowed it) but cannot add, complete, delete or move tasks. Ask in the My Plan tab or use Quick add for that; My Plan saves only after you approve.

## Does Deadline AI know today's news
No. Deadline AI cannot browse the internet, so it has no live news, prices, scores or weather. It says so and tells you where to check.

## Starting a new conversation
Press Clear in the Deadline AI window to start a new Help conversation. The assistant then forgets the earlier topic. Each person's conversations are private to their own account.
