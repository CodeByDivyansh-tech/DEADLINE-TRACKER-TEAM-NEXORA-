# Reminders and notifications

## How reminders work
Deadlines get reminders before they are due (by default 1 day and 1 hour before). To-dos with a time get a reminder 15 minutes before. Reminders appear in the bell menu at the top, and as desktop alerts if you turn those on.

## Types of notifications
Deadline tomorrow ("Your assignment deadline is tomorrow"), deadline approaching ("Your project deadline is approaching, due in two days"), overdue alerts, a daily summary ("You have three pending tasks today"), and a message when you have completed all your high-priority tasks for today.

## Notification settings
Open Settings, Reminders. Choose which reminder times you want, and turn Overdue alerts, the Daily summary, the morning reminder on the due day and the "all high-priority tasks done" message on or off. Quiet hours stop pop-up reminders between the times you choose.

## Desktop alerts
Settings, Reminders, Desktop alerts turns on browser notifications. Your browser asks for permission the first time.

## Do reminders work when the app is closed
With an account on the Deadline Tracker server, yes: the server's reminder job creates each reminder at the right time in your time zone even while the app is closed, and it waits in the bell menu for you. Pop-up desktop alerts can only appear while the app is open in a browser tab. Without the server, reminders only appear while the app is open. Email and phone push notifications aren't set up.

## Time zone
Reminders follow your account's time zone. It is set from your device when you log in, so a 5:00 pm deadline reminds you relative to 5:00 pm where you are.

## Marking notifications as read
Open the bell menu and press Mark all read, or dismiss one notification at a time.
