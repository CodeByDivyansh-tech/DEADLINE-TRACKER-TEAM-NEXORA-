# Deep Focus study sessions

## What Deep Focus is
Deep Focus is a study timer in the Study Planner (My Planner for companies) that counts only real focus time. Pick a task, how long you want to study (25, 45, 60 or 90 minutes, or Other for 5 to 240 minutes), and Normal Focus or Strict Focus, then press Start. The session opens in its own screen with the subject, the task, an optional topic, a large countdown with a progress ring, the time you started and the time you will finish.

## Normal Focus
In Normal Focus the timer pauses by itself when you leave the page (another tab, another app, the phone screen turning off) and carries on when you come back, with a short "Welcome back" note. You can pause, resume, end early, use fullscreen if you like, and minimise the session to a small timer in the corner while you use the rest of the app.

## Strict Focus
Strict Focus first shows what will happen and asks you to commit. Then the page goes fullscreen and hides everything else in the app. If you switch tabs, open another app or window, reload the page or leave fullscreen, the timer stops and the interruption is recorded. When you come back you see "You left your study session!" with the number of interruptions, the time of the last one, the total time away and the time left. The timer starts again only when you press Resume focus (which takes you back into fullscreen). Before closing or reloading during Strict Focus, the browser asks whether you want to leave.

## Can Deep Focus block other tabs or apps
No. A website is not allowed to lock your computer or phone, so Deep Focus cannot stop you opening other tabs, apps or websites. It notices when you leave, stops the timer and records it, so your study time stays honest. Real blocking would need a separate desktop app, a browser extension or a managed device. On iPhone and some other browsers fullscreen is not available; Strict Focus then still stops the timer whenever you leave the page.

## Pausing, ending early and completing
Pause stops the timer; paused minutes are break time, not study time. End early asks you to confirm and keeps the minutes you focused. When the countdown reaches zero the session is completed and you see "Excellent work! You completed your study session." with the subject, task, planned and focused time, break time, time away, interruptions and status. Finished sessions can't be started again. The focused minutes are added to the task once.

## Breaks
After a session you can press Take a short break (5, 10 or 15 minutes) or Start another session. Nothing starts by itself: Start another session takes you back to the setup with the same choices, and you press Start when you are ready.

## Refreshing, closing the browser and other devices
With an account, sessions are saved on the server. If you refresh the page, the session comes back. If you close the browser or the device turns off, the timer stops at the last moment the page was open and the session waits for you, paused, the next time you open Deadline Tracker on any device. Only one focus session can be open at a time. A session left unused for 12 hours is closed and counts as ended early.

## Focus statistics
The Study Planner shows your focus statistics from real sessions only: focused time today, this week and of all time, completed and ended-early sessions, planned versus actual time, your study streak (days in a row with focus time), how many of the last 7 days you studied, interruptions and time away, a bar for each of the last 7 days, time per subject and your recent sessions. Reports shows a shorter version. Each person only ever sees their own sessions and statistics.
