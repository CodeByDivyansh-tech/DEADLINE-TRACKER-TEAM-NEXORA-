# Daily tasks, sounds and display settings

## What the Daily tasks page is for
Daily tasks is a simple list for today's activities, like "Complete today's study plan" or "Attend the scheduled meeting". Open it from Daily tasks in the menu (Daily on a phone). It has no dates, times or reminders to fill in.

## Adding a daily task
Type a title in "What do you want to get done today?", add a short description if you like, and press Add task (or Enter). New daily tasks go on today's list.

## Ticking, unticking, editing and deleting daily tasks
Tick the square box to mark a task completed; it is crossed out. Tick it again if you marked it by mistake. Press the pencil button to change the title or description, then Save. Press the bin button to delete it; Undo appears for a few seconds.

## Progress and counts on the Daily tasks page
The top of the page shows how many tasks are pending and completed, and a progress bar for today, for example "Progress: 1 of 4 completed — 25%".

## Filters on the Daily tasks page
All shows every daily task. Today shows tasks for today. Pending shows tasks not done yet. Completed shows finished tasks.

## Are my daily tasks saved and private?
Yes. When you are signed in, daily tasks are saved to your account, so they are still there after you refresh, log out or switch devices. Only you can see them.

## Sound effects
The app plays soft clicks and short chimes when you press buttons, finish a task or something goes wrong. Sounds start only after your first click or key press, and never while you type. To turn them off, go to Settings, Profile, and switch off Sound effects. The choice is saved on that device.

## Currency in the assistant's answers
Money in Deadline AI's answers is shown in Indian Rupees by default, written like ₹1,50,000. To use another currency, go to Settings, Profile, and choose it under "Currency for money in answers".

## Light and dark mode
Press the sun or moon button at the top of the page to switch between light and dark mode. The app remembers your choice.
