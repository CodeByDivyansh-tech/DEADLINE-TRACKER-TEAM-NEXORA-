# Deadline AI assistant

## What Deadline AI can do
Deadline AI has two parts. Help answers questions about how to use Deadline Tracker, using this help guide. My Plan helps with your own work: turning what you say into tasks, planning today or tomorrow, prioritising, breaking big tasks into steps and moving unfinished work.

## Opening Deadline AI
Press the round Deadline AI button at the bottom right of any page. Choose the Help tab or the My Plan tab. The Assistant page in the sidebar is the larger version of My Plan.

## The AI to-do list generator
In My Plan, describe your work in one message, for example "Tomorrow I have to finish my Java assignment, practise DSA for two hours and submit the project report". The assistant suggests tasks with days, priorities and reminders. Nothing is saved until you press Save to my tasks. You can edit or untick suggestions first.

## Plan my day or plan tomorrow
Ask "plan my tomorrow" or "what should I complete today?". The assistant builds the plan from your saved tasks: overdue work first, then deadlines due that day or the next, then work set aside for bigger deadlines. It shows the reason for each item and warns if the day has more hours than usual.

## Does the assistant invent dates or tasks
No. It never invents deadlines, dates or details you did not give, and it never creates, changes or deletes anything without your confirmation. If something is unclear it asks you.

## Hinglish and spelling mistakes
You can write in English or Hinglish, for example "kal Java assignment submit karna hai" or "aaj ka plan bana do". Common spelling mistakes like "tommorow" are understood.

## AI on and AI off
Help uses Google Gemini (free tier) to write answers from this help guide when the server has a GEMINI_API_KEY. Without a key, Help shows the matching part of this guide directly. My Plan never uses an outside AI: it runs on the Deadline Tracker server with planning rules and reads only your own tasks. Each answer is labelled so you know where it came from.

## What is sent to Google Gemini
Only your Help question, the recent Help conversation and the matching parts of this public help guide. Your tasks, deadlines and schedule are never sent. Google may use free-tier requests to improve its products, so don't type private details into Help.

## Clearing the conversation
Press Clear in the Deadline AI window to delete the conversation. Your saved tasks are not affected.

## Rating answers
Press thumbs up or thumbs down under a Help answer. Ratings help the admin improve the help guide.
