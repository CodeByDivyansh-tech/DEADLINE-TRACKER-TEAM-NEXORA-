# Voice in Deadline AI

## Speaking to Deadline AI
Open Deadline AI and press the microphone button next to the message box. The first time, your browser asks for permission to use the microphone. While it says "Listening…", speak normally; your words appear as you talk. Press Done and the words are put in the message box so you can check or correct them, then press Send. Press Cancel to throw the words away. The microphone is only on between pressing the button and Done or Cancel. No sound recording is saved.

## Adding tasks by voice
Say what you need, for example "Add chemistry revision for tomorrow at 5 PM and Java practice for Saturday" or "Remind me to complete my Java assignment on Friday at 6 PM". Deadline AI shows a small card with each task, its date, time and priority. Press "Confirm and add" to save the tasks to your to-do list, "Edit details" to change the name, date, time or priority first, or Cancel. Nothing is saved until you confirm. You can type the same sentences instead of speaking.

## Dates and times you can say
Today, tomorrow, day after tomorrow, a weekday like Friday, this Friday, next Monday, next week, a date like 12 November, a time like 6 PM, and "in two hours". If you do not say a day or a time, the task gets none; Deadline AI never makes one up.

## Changing a task by voice
Say "Mark the Java assignment as done", "Move the Java assignment to Sunday" or "Make my Java assignment high priority". Deadline AI finds the task on your own list and shows the change to confirm. Moving a task plans it for that day; a hard deadline stays where it is.

## If the command is unclear
If Deadline AI cannot tell which task you mean, for example "Add it for Friday", it asks one short question instead of guessing. A task that is already on your list for the same day is not added twice.

## Hearing answers read aloud
Press Listen under an answer to hear it. Press Pause to pause, Resume to continue and Stop to end. To have short answers read aloud by themselves, turn on "Read answers aloud" in the Help tab. Long answers are never read out by themselves; press Listen for those.

## Which browsers support voice
Voice input uses the browser's own speech recognition. It works in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge on computers, Chrome on Android, and Safari on iPhone and iPad (recent versions). Firefox does not support it. If your browser cannot do it, the microphone button tells you so; on a phone you can still tap the microphone key on the keyboard and speak into the message box. The browser may send your speech to its maker (Google or Apple) to turn it into text.

## The microphone is blocked
If you see "The microphone is blocked for this site", press the lock or settings icon next to the web address, allow the microphone, then press the microphone button again. You can keep typing meanwhile.

## What is sent to Google Gemini when I speak a task
The sentence you said or typed is sent to Google Gemini so it can pick out the task name and the day and time words. Your saved tasks are not sent for this. Dates are worked out by Deadline Tracker itself, and tasks are saved on the Deadline Tracker server only after you confirm.
