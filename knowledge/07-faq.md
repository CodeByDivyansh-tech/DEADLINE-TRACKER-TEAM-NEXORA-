# Frequently asked questions

## How do I create a task?
Type it in the Add box on the Today page, for example "Buy notes tomorrow", and press Add. Or press N for Quick add, or ask Deadline AI in My Plan.

## How do I delete a task?
Press the bin (trash) button on the task's row. You can press Undo straight after if it was a mistake.

## How do I mark a task as complete?
Tick the round box next to it. It moves to the Done tab.

## How do I change a deadline?
Press the pencil button on the deadline, change the due date, and save. The app never changes a deadline by itself.

## How do I set a reminder?
Deadlines get reminders automatically. To change the default times open Settings, Reminders. A to-do with a time gets a reminder 15 minutes before.

## Why didn't I get a reminder?
Reminders appear while the app is open in a browser tab. Check Settings, Reminders: quiet hours may be on, or that reminder type may be switched off. Missed reminders show in the bell menu.

## How do I change my password?
Changing a password inside the app is not available yet. Ask the admin of your Deadline Tracker server for help.

## Can I use Deadline Tracker on my phone?
Yes. Open the same link in your phone's browser. The layout changes for small screens, with the main pages in a bar at the bottom.

## Does Deadline Tracker sync with Google Calendar?
There is no live sync yet. You can export your deadlines to any calendar app with Settings, Data, Export to calendar (.ics).

## Is Deadline Tracker free?
Yes. The app is free to run, and Help uses Google Gemini's free tier, with daily limits set by the server. When the daily AI limit is reached, Help still answers from the help guide.

## Why didn't Deadline AI answer my question?
Help only answers questions about using Deadline Tracker (from the help guide) and general tips on planning your time. If the guide doesn't cover something, it says it doesn't know instead of guessing, and the admin sees the question so the guide can be improved. Questions about your own tasks go to the My Plan tab.

## Do reminders work when the app is closed?
Yes, when you use the Deadline Tracker server with an account. The server creates reminders at the right time in your time zone even while the app is closed, and they wait in the bell menu. Pop-up desktop alerts appear only while the app is open in a browser tab. Email and phone notifications aren't set up.
