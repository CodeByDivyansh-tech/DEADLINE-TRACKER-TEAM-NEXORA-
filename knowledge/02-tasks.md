# Tasks and the Add box

## How to add a task or deadline
Type in the Add box on the Today page and press Add, or press the N key anywhere for Quick add. Write it the way you would say it, for example "Essay due Fri 5pm" or "Call mom tomorrow". If you write no date, the task is planned for today.

## To-do or deadline
Say "due" or "deadline" (or name an exam, quiz or viva) to create a deadline with a hard due date, for example "DBMS assignment due Friday 5pm". Anything else is a to-do planned for a day, for example "buy notes tomorrow". A task can have both a day you plan to work on it and a deadline.

## Adding several tasks at once
Separate tasks with commas or new lines: "DBMS assignment due Fri 5pm, buy notes, call Priya tomorrow" creates three items. A date said once applies to the whole list.

## Shortcuts you can type in the Add box
Dates: today, tomorrow, tonight, day after tomorrow, a weekday like Friday, next Monday, end of week, this weekend, 5 Nov or 2026-11-05.
Times: 5pm, 17:30, noon, midnight, morning (9:00), afternoon (15:00), evening (18:00), eod.
Effort: ~2h or 30 min, for example "report ~3h".
Priority: !high, urgent or important for high; !low for low.
Repeat: every day, every week, every Monday, monthly.
Subject or project: #dbms puts it in that subject or project.
Person (Company workspaces): @rahul, or "assign the slides to Rahul".

## Completing a task
Tick the round box next to the task. It moves to the Done tab. Untick it to bring it back.

## Editing a task
Press the pencil button on the task row to change its title, day, time, deadline, priority, estimate or notes, then save.

## Deleting a task
Press the bin (trash) button on the task row. An Undo button appears for a few seconds in case it was a mistake.

## Moving a task to tomorrow
Press the arrow button on the task row to move it to tomorrow. Moving a task only changes the day you plan to work on it; its deadline never moves.

## Today, Upcoming and Done tabs
The task list on the Today page has three tabs. Today shows what is planned or due today. A task you do not finish on its day is not carried to the next day: it stays on that day and shows as Missed in History (on the Daily tasks page) and in the Calendar, where you can open it to finish it or move it to another day. Overdue deadlines still show on Today. Upcoming shows future days. Done shows finished tasks.

## Searching
Use the Search box at the top to find tasks and deadlines by title.
