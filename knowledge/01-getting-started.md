# Getting started with Deadline Tracker

## What Deadline Tracker does
Deadline Tracker is a planner that tells you whether you will finish on time, not only what is due. It warns you before you fall behind, shows the reason, and helps you plan the hours backwards from each due date. It works for students, companies (teams) and personal life.

## Creating an account and logging in
When the app runs on the Deadline Tracker server, open the link and choose Create account. Enter your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters. Next time choose Log in with the same email and password. Your tasks are saved to your account on the server, so you see the same list after logging in again. To log out, open Settings, then Profile, and press Log out.

## Is my data private
Yes. Each account only sees its own tasks, deadlines and chats. Passwords are stored as a secure hash, never as plain text. The AI key stays on the server and is never sent to the browser.

## Bringing tasks from this browser into your account
If you used Deadline Tracker in this browser before creating an account, the first time you log in it asks whether to bring those tasks into your account. Choose Bring them in to copy them, or Start fresh to leave them in the browser.

## Workspaces: Student, Company and Personal
During setup you choose Student, Company or Personal. A Student workspace has Exams, a Study Planner and subjects. A Company workspace has My Work, Team Deadlines, Workload and Needs attention for managers. A Personal workspace keeps life admin like bills and appointments. You can add another workspace in Settings, Profile, New workspace.

## The sidebar and pages
The sidebar shows Today, Assistant, Calendar and Deadlines. More opens the other pages: Exams, Study Planner and Reports for students, plus Settings. On a phone the same pages are in the bar at the bottom.

## Sample data
New workspaces can start with sample deadlines so you can try things. A Sample data badge shows at the top. Press Clear next to it, or Settings, Profile, Clear sample data, to remove them.

## Light and dark theme
Press the moon or sun button at the top right to switch between light and dark theme.
